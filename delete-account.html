How to delete your account

Send an email to indrekl@gmail.com with the subject Delete my Spino Ghost Racing account and your racer name as shown in the game's garage. We delete the account within 30 days and confirm by reply.

What is deleted

The account, your racer name, your best times and replays on every leaderboard, your championship entries and results, the championships you made, your sponsor boards, and your reports. Tracks you published are taken down from the community feed.

What is kept

Purchase records held by Google Play, the App Store and RevenueCat under their own terms, and a log line of moderation actions taken on the account, kept without your name. Nothing else.

Without deleting the account

There is no separate way to delete part of your data. Write to the same address with what you want removed and we will do it by hand.
