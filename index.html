Questions

Is Spino Ghost Racing free?

Yes. It is free to download and play, with no ads. The only purchases are optional: paint for your car and sponsor boards that put your name on a track.

Who am I racing against?

Ghosts: recorded laps from other players on the same track, replayed exactly as they were driven. Beat one and your lap becomes a ghost for the next player to chase.

Can I make my own race tracks?

Yes. Draw a circuit in the track editor, drive a proof lap to show it can be finished, and publish it for everyone to race.

Is it pay-to-win?

No. Speed is never for sale. Paint and sponsor boards change how things look, never how fast you go.

Do I need an account?

No sign-in and no email. The game makes an account for your phone the first time you open it.

What can I play Spino Ghost Racing on?

Android phones and tablets, from Google Play. iPhone and iPad are coming soon on the App Store. Play with touch controls, a keyboard or a gamepad.
