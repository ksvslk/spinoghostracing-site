Five reports from different players take a piece down automatically pending review.

Times count only when the game's own replay of your lap checks out on our server. Modified clients, replayed or shared recordings, and anything else that fakes a lap will be refused, and repeat attempts get the account banned.

7. Your data

We store: an account id, your racer name, your car's look, your best times and their replays, the tracks and cups you publish, reports you file, and purchase records from the store. Times and replays are public on the boards under your racer name. We do not collect your email, contacts or location. Purchases are processed by RevenueCat and the store. Data is kept on Supabase in the EU. The game also sends anonymous usage statistics to Google Analytics for Firebase — which screens are opened, races finished, purchases made — under a random install id, never your name or account. You can switch this off at any time in Settings › Usage statistics. Notifications are delivered by OneSignal, which holds your account id, your device's push token and a few counts (races, tracks published, purchases) used to decide what to send; nothing you type. The system's notification setting and Settings › Daily track alert control them. To have what you published taken down, or to delete the account entirely, contact us.
