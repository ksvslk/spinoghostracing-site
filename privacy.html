Privacy policy

Spino Ghost Racing · September 2026

What we store

We store: an account id, your racer name, your car's look, your best times and their replays, the tracks and cups you publish, reports you file, and purchase records from the store. Times and replays are public on the boards under your racer name. We do not collect your email, contacts or location. Purchases are processed by RevenueCat and the store. Data is kept on Supabase in the EU. To have what you published taken down, or to delete the account entirely, contact us.

Why

To show your racer name and times on the leaderboards, to run championships, to keep the tracks and cups you publish available to other players, to deliver what you bought, and to act on reports of abuse.

What we do not collect

No email address, no contacts, no location, no advertising identifiers, no analytics beyond the store's own install statistics. There is no third-party advertising in the game.

Who else sees it

Your racer name, your times and replays, and the tracks and cups you publish are public inside the game. Purchases are processed by Google Play or the App Store and by RevenueCat. Data is stored on Supabase in the European Union (Frankfurt). Names you type are checked by an automated moderation service (OpenAI's moderation model) before they are shown to others; the name is the only thing sent.

How long

For as long as your account exists. See Delete your account for how to end it.

Children

The game is rated for players 13 and over. We do not knowingly keep data of younger children; if you believe we do, contact us and we will delete it.

Changes

When this policy changes the game shows the new version of the terms and asks again.
